# Gym Owner Interview Guide
## Customer Discovery for Gym Management Software

---

## Overview

**Goal:** Understand gym owners' real problems, current solutions, and willingness to pay for better software.

**Target:** 10-15 gym owners in Salem/Portland area

**Time per interview:** 20-30 minutes

**Format:** In-person coffee (best) or phone/Zoom

## Interview Structure (20-30 minutes)

### 1. Intro (2 minutes)
- Thank them for time
- Explain you're researching gym software
- Emphasize you're not selling anything TODAY
- Ask permission to take notes

### 2. Background (3-5 minutes)
- Learn about their gym
- Build rapport
- Context for later questions

### 3. Current Process (5-8 minutes)
- How they manage now
- What tools they use
- Daily workflows

### 4. Pain Points (5-8 minutes)
- What frustrates them
- What takes too much time
- What they wish existed

### 5. Existing Solutions (3-5 minutes)
- Have they tried software?
- Why did they stick with it or leave?
- What do they pay now?

### 6. Willingness to Pay (3-5 minutes)
- Budget questions
- What they'd pay for the right solution

### 7. Wrap-Up (2 minutes)
- Thank them
- Offer to share findings
- Ask for referrals to other gym owners

## The Questions

### PART 1: Background & Context

**Goal:** Build rapport, understand their business

#### Q1: Tell me about your gym
**Follow-ups:**
- How long have you been running it?
- What type of gym is it? (CrossFit, general fitness, boutique, etc.)
- How many members do you have?
- Is it just you or do you have staff?

**What to listen for:**
- Gym size (small vs large)
- Complexity (solo vs staff)
- Type (niche vs general)

---

#### Q2: What does a typical day look like for you?
**Follow-ups:**
- What tasks take up most of your time?
- What do you do first thing in the morning?
- What keeps you busy throughout the day?

**What to listen for:**
- Admin work vs member interaction
- Time wasters
- Bottlenecks

---

#### Q3: What made you decide to open a gym?
**Follow-ups:**
- Did you start from scratch or buy existing?
- What's your background?

**What to listen for:**
- Passion for fitness vs business opportunity
- Technical savviness
- Business acumen

---

### PART 2: Current Tech & Process

**Goal:** Understand what they use now and why

#### Q4: How do you currently manage members? (DATABASE)
**Follow-ups:**
- What system do you use?
- How do members check in?
- How do you track who has access?
- Do you keep notes on members?

**What to listen for:**
- Spreadsheets = opportunity
- Clunky software = frustration
- Manual processes = pain point

---

#### Q5: How do you handle billing and payments? (BILLING)
**Follow-ups:**
- What system do you use?
- How often do you bill?
- Do you have payment failures?
- How much time do you spend on billing issues each month?

**What to listen for:**
- Manual billing = huge pain
- Failed payments = revenue leakage
- Time spent = willingness to pay for automation

---

#### Q6: How do you manage class schedules? (SCHEDULING)
**Follow-ups:**
- Do you offer classes?
- How do members sign up?
- Do you have capacity limits?
- How do you handle waitlists?

**What to listen for:**
- Phone calls/texts for booking = manual pain
- Overbooking issues = problem to solve
- No system = huge opportunity

---

#### Q7: How do you communicate with members? (COMMUNICATION)
**Follow-ups:**
- Email, text, app, in-person?
- Do you send newsletters?
- How do you announce schedule changes?
- Do members have a way to reach you?

**What to listen for:**
- Scattered communication = consolidation opportunity
- Manual announcements = automation need
- No mobile app = feature opportunity

---

#### Q8: How do you track attendance and usage? (ANALYTICS)
**Follow-ups:**
- Do you know who comes regularly vs rarely?
- Can you identify at-risk members?
- Do you track peak hours?
- How do you measure success?

**What to listen for:**
- "I don't really know" = analytics opportunity
- Manual tracking = pain point
- No insights = competitive advantage

---

#### Q9: Walk me through what happens when a new member signs up
**Follow-ups:**
- Is it in-person, online, or both?
- What paperwork is involved?
- How long does it take?
- What systems do you use?

**What to listen for:**
- Manual onboarding = friction
- Paper forms = digitization opportunity
- Multiple systems = consolidation need

---

### PART 3: Pain Points & Frustrations

**Goal:** Find the REAL problems worth solving

#### Q10: What's the most frustrating part of running your gym?
**Follow-ups:**
- If you could wave a magic wand and fix one thing, what would it be?
- What keeps you up at night?
- What makes you want to quit?

**What to listen for:**
- Technology problems vs other issues
- Severity of pain (urgent vs nice-to-have)
- Willingness to pay (desperate = higher value)

---

#### Q11: How much time do you spend on administrative tasks vs training/coaching?
**Follow-ups:**
- Would you rather spend more time with members?
- What admin tasks could be automated?
- What takes way longer than it should?

**What to listen for:**
- Time spent = value of automation
- Specific tasks mentioned = features to build
- Frustration level = urgency

---

#### Q12: What do your members complain about most?
**Follow-ups:**
- Booking issues?
- Billing issues?
- Communication issues?
- App/website issues?

**What to listen for:**
- Member experience problems = your UX opportunity
- Recurring complaints = must-fix features
- Lost members due to tech = revenue impact

---

#### Q13: Have you ever lost members due to technology issues?
**Follow-ups:**
- What happened?
- Could better software have prevented it?
- How many members?

**What to listen for:**
- Revenue impact of bad tech
- Real consequences = willingness to pay
- Specific failure points

---

### PART 4: Existing Software Experience

**Goal:** Understand what they've tried and why it failed/succeeded

#### Q14: Are you currently using any gym management software?

**If YES:**
- Which one?
- How long have you used it?
- What do you like about it?
- What drives you crazy about it?
- How much do you pay per month?
- Have you considered switching? Why or why not?

**If NO:**
- Have you tried any in the past?
- Why didn't you stick with it?
- What made you go back to [spreadsheets/manual]?
- What would it take for you to try software again?

**What to listen for:**
- Switching barriers (data migration, learning curve, cost)
- Deal-breakers (must-not-haves)
- Unmet needs from current solution
- Price sensitivity

---

#### Q15: What do you love about your current solution?
**Follow-ups:**
- What would you be sad to lose if you switched?
- What features do you use every day?
- What makes your life easier?

**What to listen for:**
- Must-have features (can't launch without these)
- What NOT to change
- Core value proposition of competitors

---

#### Q16: If you could add one feature to your current setup, what would it be?
**Follow-ups:**
- Why don't you have that now?
- How much would that help?
- Would you pay extra for it?

**What to listen for:**
- Unmet needs = your opportunity
- Feature requests = product roadmap
- Value of specific features

---

#### Q17: What almost made you choose a different software?
**Follow-ups:**
- What other options did you consider?
- What made you pick the one you did?
- Any regrets?

**What to listen for:**
- Decision criteria (price, features, support)
- Competitive positioning
- What matters most

---

### PART 5: Budget & Willingness to Pay

**Goal:** Understand pricing and validate business model

#### Q18: How much do you currently spend on software/tech for your gym?
**Follow-ups:**
- Gym management software?
- Payment processing?
- Email/marketing tools?
- Website?
- Other subscriptions?

**What to listen for:**
- Total tech budget
- Where they're willing to spend
- Price sensitivity
- Current spend = anchor for pricing

---

#### Q19: If the perfect gym management software existed, what would you be willing to pay per month?
**Follow-ups:**
- What if it saved you 10 hours per month?
- What if it helped you keep 5 more members per month?
- Would you pay more for local support?
- Flat rate vs per-member pricing?

**What to listen for:**
- Price range comfort zone
- Value-based pricing opportunities
- Flat vs usage-based preference

---

#### Q20: What would make you switch from your current solution?
**Follow-ups:**
- Better price?
- Specific features?
- Better support?
- Easier to use?
- How much better/cheaper would it need to be?

**What to listen for:**
- Switching motivation
- Competitive differentiation needed
- Degree of improvement required

---

#### Q21: Have you ever NOT bought software because of the price?
**Follow-ups:**
- Which software?
- What was too expensive?
- What would have been a fair price?

**What to listen for:**
- Price ceiling
- Competitor pricing problems
- Your pricing opportunity

---

### PART 6: Future & Ideal Solution

**Goal:** Understand their perfect solution

#### Q22: If you could design the perfect gym management system, what would it do?
**Follow-ups:**
- What features are must-haves?
- What would be nice-to-haves?
- What would you never use?

**What to listen for:**
- Feature priorities
- MVP vs future features
- What to NOT build

---

#### Q23: How important is mobile access (for you and members)?
**Follow-ups:**
- Do your members want an app?
- Do you manage things from your phone?
- What would you do on mobile vs desktop?

**What to listen for:**
- Mobile-first opportunity
- Member app value
- Admin mobile needs

---

#### Q24: How important is local support vs cheaper offshore support?
**Follow-ups:**
- Have you had bad support experiences?
- Would you pay more for someone local?
- Do you prefer phone, email, or in-person help?

**What to listen for:**
- Support as differentiator
- Local advantage value
- Support expectations

---

#### Q25: Would you be interested in beta testing new gym software?
**Follow-ups:**
- What would you need to see first?
- Would you be willing to give feedback?
- Could I follow up with you as I develop this?

**What to listen for:**
- Potential beta customers
- Early adopter mindset
- Permission to follow up

---

### PART 7: Wrap-Up & Referrals

#### Q26: Is there anything else you think I should know about running a gym or gym software?
**Follow-ups:**
- Any other frustrations?
- Any other tools you wish existed?

**What to listen for:**
- Things you didn't ask about
- Adjacent problems
- Broader context

---

#### Q27: Do you know other gym owners I should talk to?
**Follow-ups:**
- Anyone in Salem/Portland area?
- Anyone who's particularly frustrated with their current setup?
- Could you introduce me?

**What to listen for:**
- Referrals = warm intros
- Network access
- Specific recommendations

---

#### Q28: Can I follow up with you as I develop this?
**Follow-ups:**
- Would you be interested in early access?
- Can I email you in a few months?
- What's the best way to reach you?

**What to listen for:**
- Future customer pipeline
- Feedback loop
- Beta tester potential

## How to Conduct the Interview

### DO:
✅ **Listen more than talk** (80/20 rule)
✅ **Ask "why" and "tell me more"** (dig deeper)
✅ **Stay neutral** (don't pitch your solution)
✅ **Take detailed notes** (or record with permission)
✅ **Be genuinely curious** (people can tell if you care)
✅ **Ask for specific examples** ("Tell me about the last time that happened")
✅ **Probe for emotion** ("How did that make you feel?")
✅ **Follow the energy** (spend time on topics they're passionate about)

### DON'T:
❌ **Don't pitch your solution** (you're learning, not selling)
❌ **Don't lead the witness** ("Wouldn't it be great if...")
❌ **Don't ask yes/no questions** (ask open-ended)
❌ **Don't interrupt** (let them finish)
❌ **Don't talk about features** (talk about problems)
❌ **Don't defend your ideas** (you're wrong until proven right)
❌ **Don't skip the awkward silence** (let them think)

## What You're Looking For

### Strong Signals (Green Lights):

✅ **"I hate [current software] because..."**
- Strong emotion = real pain

✅ **"I spend 10 hours a week on..."**
- Quantified pain = measurable value

✅ **"I'd pay $X for something that..."**
- Willingness to pay = validated demand

✅ **"We lost members because..."**
- Revenue impact = high-value problem

✅ **"I tried [competitor] but..."**
- Tried competitors = active searcher

✅ **"When can I get this?"**
- Eager for solution = ready buyer

### Weak Signals (Yellow/Red Flags):

⚠️ **"That would be nice..."**
- Nice-to-have, not must-have

⚠️ **"I'd have to think about it..."**
- Not urgent

⚠️ **"I'm pretty happy with [current solution]"**
- Low switching motivation

⚠️ **"I can't afford to spend more on software"**
- Budget constraints

⚠️ **"My members don't really use tech"**
- Market mismatch

❌ **"That's an interesting idea, but..."**
- Polite rejection

## After 10 Interviews

### Analysis Questions:

1. **What problems came up in 70%+ of interviews?**
   - These are validated problems

2. **What's the most painful problem?**
   - Measure by emotion + time + money impact

3. **What are people currently paying?**
   - Establishes pricing range

4. **What did people say they'd pay?**
   - Usually lower than reality, but directional

5. **Which gym type had the most pain?**
   - Your target market

6. **What features were mentioned most?**
   - Your MVP feature list

7. **Who said "I'd buy this tomorrow"?**
   - Your first beta customers

8. **What surprised you?**
   - Often the most valuable insight

## Decision Framework

### You Have Product-Market Fit Signals If:

✅ **5+ gym owners have the SAME main problem**
✅ **3+ would pay $50+ per month to solve it**
✅ **2+ would beta test for free/cheap**
✅ **People got excited talking about the problem**
✅ **Current solutions are clearly inadequate**
✅ **You can articulate the problem better than they can**

### You Should Pivot If:

❌ **Every gym has different problems**
❌ **No one would pay what you need to charge**
❌ **People are happy with current solutions**
❌ **The problem isn't painful enough**
❌ **No one wants to beta test**

## Sample Interview in Action

### Example Exchange:

**You:** "Tell me about your gym."

**Owner:** "We're a small CrossFit box, about 60 members. Been running it for 3 years."

**You:** "What does a typical day look like for you?"

**Owner:** "Ugh, mornings are crazy. I'm coaching the 6am class, then I have to run to my computer to process billing that failed overnight, then back for the 9am class..."

🔥 **PROBE DEEPER:**

**You:** "Tell me more about billing that fails overnight. How often does that happen?"

**Owner:** "Maybe 3-5 failures per week. Credit cards expired, insufficient funds, whatever. I have to manually email each person, update their payment info, reprocess... takes 20-30 minutes each time."

💰 **QUANTIFY THE PAIN:**

**You:** "So roughly 2-3 hours per week just on failed payments?"

**Owner:** "Yeah, easily. And members get annoyed when I bug them about it."

✅ **VALIDATE WILLINGNESS TO PAY:**

**You:** "If software could automatically retry failed payments and send members a text to update their card info, how much time would that save you?"

**Owner:** "Oh man, all of it. That would be amazing."

**You:** "Would that be worth $20-30 per month to you?"

**Owner:** "Absolutely. Where do I sign up?"

## Scripts for Tricky Situations

### If they ask what you're building:

**Bad:** "I'm building gym management software."
- Now they'll critique your idea instead of sharing problems

**Good:** "I'm exploring problems gym owners face with technology. I don't have a solution yet - I'm just trying to understand the space. That's why your input is so valuable."

---

### If they try to pitch you their ideas:

**Response:** "That's interesting! Tell me more about why that would help you. What problem would that solve?"
- Redirect from solution back to problem

---

### If they ask about pricing:

**Response:** "I honestly don't know yet - that's one reason I'm doing these interviews. What would you expect to pay for something that [solves their problem]?"

---

### If they're not tech-savvy:

**Response:** "That's actually really helpful to know. Walk me through how you do [task] right now without software."
- Manual processes are where the biggest opportunities are

## Next Steps After Interviews

### 1. Compile Findings (Week 1)
- Aggregate all notes
- Identify top 3 problems
- Define target customer (gym type, size, location)

### 2. Validate Further (Week 2)
- Interview 5 more gyms specifically of target type
- Confirm problem is consistent
- Refine understanding

### 3. Design MVP (Week 3)
- List must-have features (top 5)
- Design wireframes
- Validate with 2-3 potential customers

### 4. Build Beta (Weeks 4-8)
- Build minimal viable product
- Focus only on core problem
- Get 2-3 beta customers committed

### 5. Launch & Iterate (Week 9+)
- Launch to beta customers
- Get feedback weekly
- Iterate quickly
- Add customers gradually

## Key Principles

### Mom Test Principles:

1. **Talk about their life, not your idea**
2. **Ask about specifics in the past, not generics or the future**
3. **Listen more than talk**

### Bad Question Examples:

❌ "Would you use software that does X?"
- People lie about future behavior

❌ "Is this a good idea?"
- People are too polite

❌ "Would you pay $99/month for this?"
- Hypothetical pricing is unreliable

### Good Question Examples:

✅ "Tell me about the last time you had to [do painful task]"
- Specific past behavior

✅ "How much time did that take?"
- Quantifiable pain

✅ "What have you tried to solve this?"
- Reveals existing alternatives and switching cost

## Red Flags to Watch For

### Interview Red Flags:

🚩 **They immediately start pitching you their "million dollar idea"**
- They're not your customer

🚩 **They say "everyone needs this!"**
- Too broad, not validated

🚩 **They can't articulate specific pain points**
- Problem not painful enough

🚩 **They're "too busy" to talk but "very interested"**
- Low priority for them = not worth solving

🚩 **They want equity/partnership instead of paying**
- Not willing to pay = weak validation

### Business Model Red Flags:

🚩 **10 interviews, 10 different problems**
- No clear target market

🚩 **"I'd pay $10/month max"**
- Can't build sustainable business

🚩 **"I'm happy with [free solution]"**
- No willingness to pay

🚩 **"My members would never use an app"**
- Market not ready
