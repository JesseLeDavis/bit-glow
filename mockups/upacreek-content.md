## Recommended clean layout (the whole point of the redesign)

The current site buries the good stuff in clutter. New homepage, top to bottom:

1. **Sticky header** — logo left · phone (click-to-call) + "Request a Quote" button right
2. **Hero** — headline "Residential Plumbing. Reliable Service. Reasonable Price." · tagline · one strong CTA · a trust strip underneath ("Licensed · Bonded · Insured · CCB #200407")
3. **Trust bar** — award badges + "A rating · 300+ reviews" as a clean single row (currently scattered — consolidate)
4. **Services** — tidy grid of the service list, grouped (Repairs / Installations / Specialty), not one long dumped list
5. **Before & After** — 2–3 clean image pairs, big and uncluttered
6. **Meet the team** — Shaun & Sarah, one photo + one line each (small business = trust)
7. **Reviews** — pull 2–3 real quotes from their Google/Yelp
8. **Service area** — "Serving Salem, Keizer & surrounding areas" + simple map
9. **Footer CTA** — phone + quote button repeated · updated copyright

**Cut / declutter:** the Einstein quote, redundant repeated CTAs mid-page, and any 2018-era styling. Keep it to one clear action per screen: call or request a quote.
