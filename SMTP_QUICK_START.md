# SMTP - Super Quick Setup (3 Steps!)

---

## Step 1: Add Your Password (30 seconds)

1. **Open** `contact-smtp.php` in your code editor
2. **Find line 30** - it says: `YOUR_EMAIL_PASSWORD_HERE`
3. **Replace it** with your `noreply@bitglow.tech` email password
4. **Save**

**Don't know the password?**
- hPanel → Emails → noreply@bitglow.tech → Manage → Change Password

---

## Step 2: Rename & Upload (1 minute)

1. **Rename** the file: `contact-smtp.php` → `contact.php`
2. **Upload** to Hostinger File Manager (same folder as index.html)
3. **Replace** the old contact.php when asked

---

## Step 3: Test (30 seconds)

1. **Go to** https://bitglow.tech
2. **Fill out** contact form
3. **Submit**
4. **Check** your email!

---

## ✅ Done!

Emails going to spam? Add noreply@bitglow.tech to your contacts!

**See SMTP_SETUP.md for detailed help**

---

**Total Time: 2 minutes**
