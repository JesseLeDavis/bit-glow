# BitGlow - Salem, Oregon Business Outreach Plan

## Executive Summary
BitGlow is targeting small to medium businesses in Salem, OR that need modern web and mobile solutions. Our advantage: we're local, affordable, and we actually care about helping Salem businesses grow.

---

## Target Market Analysis

### Salem Business Landscape
- Population: ~175,000 (3rd largest city in Oregon)
- Strong small business community
- Many businesses still have outdated or no websites
- Growing need for online ordering, booking systems, mobile apps

### Primary Targets

#### 1. Small Retail Shops
**Why:** Need e-commerce, better online presence
**Pain Points:**
- Losing sales to Amazon/big retailers
- Outdated or no website
- No online shopping capability
**Examples in Salem:**
- Downtown boutiques on Liberty St
- Specialty shops in Reed Opera House
- Local shops on Commercial St

#### 2. Restaurants & Cafes
**Why:** High demand for online ordering, reservation systems
**Pain Points:**
- Paying high fees to DoorDash/UberEats
- No direct online ordering
- Outdated menus/websites
**Examples in Salem:**
- Independent restaurants downtown
- Food trucks wanting ordering systems
- Cafes needing loyalty programs

#### 3. Professional Services
**Why:** Need credibility, modern web presence
**Pain Points:**
- Outdated websites hurting credibility
- No online booking/scheduling
- Not mobile-friendly
**Examples in Salem:**
- Law firms
- Accounting offices
- Real estate agents
- Insurance agencies

#### 4. Healthcare & Wellness
**Why:** Growing sector, need booking systems
**Pain Points:**
- Manual scheduling/booking
- No patient portals
- Poor mobile experience
**Examples in Salem:**
- Physical therapy clinics
- Gyms/fitness studios
- Wellness centers
- Dental offices

### Phase 3: In-Person Outreach (Ongoing)

#### Walk-In Strategy
**Best for:** Retail, restaurants, small offices

**Approach:**
1. Visit during slow hours (Tue-Thu, 2-4pm)
2. Dress professionally but approachable
3. Bring business cards (design one that matches your terminal theme!)
4. Have your phone ready to show your website

**Script:**
```
"Hi! I'm Jesse, I run a web development business here in Salem called BitGlow.
I was in the area and wanted to introduce myself. I help local businesses with
their websites and online presence.

[If they seem interested]
I noticed [specific observation about their business]. I'd love to show you
some examples of work I've done for other Salem businesses. Do you have a card
or email where I could send you some information?"
```

**Tips:**
- Be respectful of their time
- Don't be pushy
- Leave a card even if they're not interested
- Ask "Who handles your website/marketing?" if talking to staff

## Common Objections & How to Handle

### "We already have a website"
**Response:** "That's great! How's it performing for you? Are you happy with how many leads it brings in? I'd be happy to give you a free quick audit and point out a few quick wins."

### "We don't have budget right now"
**Response:** "I totally understand. Many Salem businesses I work with started with a payment plan. We could break it into $X per month over 5-6 months. Or I'm happy to stay in touch for when timing is better."

### "My nephew/friend does websites"
**Response:** "That's awesome that you have someone helping you! If they ever get too busy or you need additional support, I'd be happy to help. Here's my card in case it's useful down the road."

### "We're too small to need a website"
**Response:** "I hear you. But these days, 90% of people check online before visiting a business. Even a simple one-page site with your hours, location, and services can bring in 5-10 more customers a month. Would that be helpful?"

### "How do I know you're legit?"
**Response:** "Great question! Here's my website with case studies. I can also connect you with a few other Salem businesses I've worked with if you'd like to hear directly from them."
