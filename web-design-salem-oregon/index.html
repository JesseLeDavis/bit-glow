// questions

Hiring a web designer in Salem.

Don't see yours? Ask me directly.

Are you a web designer or a web developer?
Both. Design is how your site looks and reads, and development is how it's built and how reliably it works. Most small businesses need both, and with me that's one person and one flat price.
How much does a website cost?
Starter sites (1–3 pages) are $900–1,200, Standard sites (up to 5 pages) are $1,800–2,500, and Premium sites (8–12 pages with copywriting) are $3,500–5,000. If you already have a decent site, a Fix-up is $400–900. You get a flat quote after a free call.
Can you help my business show up on Google in Salem?
Yes, though nobody can honestly guarantee a ranking. Starter includes Google Business Profile cleanup and basic SEO, Standard includes local SEO and Search Console setup, and Premium includes a separate page for each service and each town you serve.
Do you work with businesses in Keizer and West Salem?
Yes. I work with businesses across Marion and Polk counties, including Keizer and West Salem. Most of the process happens over calls and email, so it works the same wherever your business is.
How long does a website take?
A typical small-business website launches 2–3 weeks after I have your content (text, photos, logo). Online stores and custom tools take longer. I'll give you a realistic timeline before we start.
