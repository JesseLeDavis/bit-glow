// questions

Straight answers.

Don't see yours? Ask me directly.

How much does a website cost?
Most small-business websites start at $1,500, online stores at $3,000, and custom booking systems or apps at $5,000. You get a clear, fixed quote after a free call, with 50% due to start and 50% before launch. No hourly surprises.
Do I have to sign up for a care plan?
No. Care plans are optional and start at $59/month for hosting, updates, backups and small edits. After the first 3 months they're month to month, and if you ever leave, you get a full backup of your site for free.
Will I own my website?
Yes. Once the project is paid, you own the design, content and code made for your site. Your domain is registered in your own name, not mine.
How long does a project take?
A typical small-business website launches 2–3 weeks after I have your content (text, photos, logo). Online stores and custom tools take longer. I'll give you a realistic timeline before we start.
Where are you located, and do you work with clients outside Salem?
I'm based in Salem, Oregon, and I love working with local businesses across Marion and Polk counties. I also work with clients remotely across the U.S. Most of the process happens over calls and email either way.
Will I work directly with the developer?
Yes, always. There are no account managers or middlemen. You talk directly to the person building your site, which means less lost in translation and faster decisions.
